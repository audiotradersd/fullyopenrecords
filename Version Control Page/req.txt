PAGE PURPOSE

Fully Open Records has a Track Version Control feature.

The central idea is that a musician can assign every version created during the life of a song to ONE track.

Instead of the first phone recording, rehearsal recording, demo, revised mix and final master becoming disconnected files, they form a chronological history of the same track.

Each version can contain:
- Audio
- Date/stage
- Notes
- Images
- Recording/setup information
- Guitar/instrument used
- Amplifier and settings
- Pedals/effects and settings
- Recording equipment
- Production/mix notes

This creates a permanent creative diary showing how a song evolved from its first idea to the finished master.

The page should communicate that Fully Open Records is unique in treating version history as part of the creative record rather than simply providing storage for finished songs.

IMPORTANT PRODUCT POSITIONING

Do not describe this simply as "file versioning".

The important concept is:

ONE TRACK
→ MANY VERSIONS
→ ONE CREATIVE HISTORY

It is effectively a diary/journal of the creation of a piece of music.

The musician should be able to look back years later and hear the first riff, see a photograph of the pedalboard they used, read their original notes, inspect their amp settings and then follow the song through rehearsals, demos, mixes and mastering.

That emotional/archival value should come through in the page.
